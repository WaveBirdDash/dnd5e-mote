## Spell Descriptions

The spells are presented in alphabetical order.

### Kyran's Gambit

_Level 1 Abjuration_ (Rogue (Arcane Trickster), Warlock)

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a drop of the target's blood, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You attempt to trap a creature you touch in a confined space with you to confront them alone. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a successful save, the spell fails.

On a failed save, for the spell's duration, both you and the target are trapped within a ring of invisible force in a 15-foot radius Sphere centered on the target's original location. When the ring of force is created, creatures other than you and the target within the area of effect are pushed in a straight line away from the center of the ring to an unoccupied space. If there is no space for such creatures to be pushed to, the spell ends.

The ring of force takes on a disturbing illusory appearance of your choice each time you create it, but this illusion does not obstruct visibility of creatures inside the area of effect. Creatures and objects are barred from entering the ring. Spells of a level lower than the spell slot level used to cast this spell can't be cast through it, and the effects of such spells can't extend into it.

A creature that starts its turn in the ring of force is wracked with pain and mental torment, taking 1d8 Psychic damage. When you take this damage, it can't be reduced or prevented in any way, but it doesn't break your Concentration to maintain this spell.

Damage dealt to a creature in the ring of force that would reduce it to 0 Hit Points, and any effect that would instantly kill or destroy a creature, instead reduces that creature to 1 Hit Point, which also ends the spell. The target also makes a Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a success. Once the spell ends, the ring of force disappears. Removing you or the target from the ring also ends the spell.

After the spell ends, or if the spell fails, the target is immune to this spell for the next 24 hours.

_**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.**_ The damage dealt by starting a turn in the ring of force increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1. Additionally, if you use a level 6+ spell slot, the spell doesn't require Concentration.

### Pass Without Note

_Level 2 Enchantment_ (Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard)

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a scrap of garment common to the current social setting and a dab of makeup applied to your face)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You radiate an aura of irrelevance in a 30-foot Emanation for the duration. While in the aura, you and each creature you choose have a +10 bonus to Charisma (Performance) checks made to be dismissed or go unnoticed socially, appearing unremarkable and uninteresting compared to other people in the setting. Creatures who fail checks to notice you have difficulty recalling anything noteworthy about your appearance or presence. Creatures who are immune to the Charmed condition ignore the spell's bonus to Charisma checks when making contested checks.
