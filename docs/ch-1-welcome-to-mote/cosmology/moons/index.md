# Moons of Mote

[Mote](../mote.md) has four natural satellites:

- [Kiva](kiva.md)
- [Bathýs-Pela](bathys-pela.md)
- [Dreg](dreg.md)

## Lunar Confluences

Very rarely, when the orbits of the moons of Mote come in close proximity, the complicated interactions of their gravity and [starstuff](../../../ch-6-mote-treasures/starstuff.md) energies can temporarily slow their movement across the sky. This confluence of heavenly bodies wreaks havoc on the surface of Mote, extending the length of the day as the world's rotation is temporarily disrupted and tidally locked by the amplified lunar forces. Due to their effect on the day-night cycle, such periods are colloquially known as "lunar days".

### Frequency

In antiquity, lunar confluences were thought to be harbingers of ill portent, with many cultures having legends about confluences preceding natural disasters or societal collapse. The last recorded Lunar confluence was several decades ago, such that only older people and species recall the events in any detail. They are considered to be quite rare in modern times, which means new confluences can take people by surprise.

The DM determines whether a lunar confluence might occur at the start of a given day, which can be done by rolling a d100. On a 1, two or more moons align their orbits and slow the rotation of Mote to a halt. A d24 can be rolled to randomize an hour of the day, or the DM can decide when it occurs.

### Duration

During a lunar day, the orbital speed of all involved moons are slowed or halted for the entirety of their interaction, as their gravitational and stellar forces drag against each other. This drag can vary in length from a few hours to over a day. A d20 can be rolled to randomize the duration of a lunar confluence in hours, or the DM can decide how long it lasts. Each time you roll a 20 on the d20, you can roll another d20 and add it to the total.

### Effects

Fueling ancient superstitions, lunar confluences have been observed to create chaotic and unpredictable magical effects. When a lunar confluence occurs, the DM chooses an effect, or can roll on the **Lunar Confluence Effects** table for a random effect, which lasts until the confluence ends and the moons continue their normal orbits around Mote. Effects marked with a dagger † can be found in the **Environmental Effects** section of the _Dungeon Master's Guide_.

When a lunar confluence occurs at night, effects that occur at dawn, such as magic items that regain expended charges, are suppressed until Mote resumes its rotation and sees the rising sun. Effects that occur after set periods of time, such as casting spells or completing a Short Rest, function normally, unless the confluence manifests effects which impact these features or abilities.
